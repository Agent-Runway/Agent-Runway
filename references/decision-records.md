# Decision Records

Use a compact decision record when ambiguity is meaningful but not approval-worthy.

## Record fields

- decision
- context
- chosen option
- rejected options
- evidence used
- reversibility
- reopen triggers

## When to record

Record a decision when:

- multiple reversible choices are plausible
- future readers may misremember why a path was chosen
- the choice could bias later interpretation of evidence
- a later rollback would be cheaper if the rationale is clear

## When not to record

Do not record trivial local choices whose rationale is obvious from the diff or receipt trail.
